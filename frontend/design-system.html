The living reference for the Claude marketing surface: every color, type style, spacing step, radius, and component, rendered with the tokens it documents.

Copernicus and StyreneB are licensed Anthropic typefaces. This page renders with their documented fallbacks: Garamond-class serifs for display, Inter-class humanist sans for body. Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.1 relative luminance against the canvas (#faf9f5).

Spacing

A 4px base unit, doubling toward a 96px section rhythm. Cards breathe at 32px inside; bands separate at 96px. Each bar below is drawn at true length.

Radius & elevation

Radius is hierarchical: 8px for controls, 12px for cards, 16px for hero containers, pill for badges. Depth is color-block first; shadows are rare.

Border radius

sm 6px · dropdown items
md 8px · buttons, inputs
lg 12px · content cards
xl 16px · hero containers
pill 9999px · badges
