Live region & pause on interact

Push toasts into a polite viewport. Auto-dismiss lasts 16× --duration-slow (read from the token at runtime). Hover or focus a toast to pause; leave to resume. Close is always available.

The viewport — not each toast — owns aria-live="polite". Screen readers hear additions without assertive interruption when several toasts land in sequence.
