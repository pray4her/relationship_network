Progress

A non-interactive progress indicator. The trough is the Switch/Slider resting-trough recipe (--surface-cream-strong, pill radius), the fill is --primary or a semantic status token when the value itself is a status, and the root carries the WAI-ARIA progressbar contract — aria-valuenow is omitted while indeterminate, exactly as the spec prescribes.

Indeterminate omits aria-valuenow (the ARIA signal for “no known value”) and sweeps a --space-16 pill across the trough at calc(--duration-slow * 4) with --ease-standard — a derivation from the existing motion token, so the three-duration doctrine holds. Under prefers-reduced-motion the segment rests at the track start.
