Lifecycle — closed · opening · open · closing

The native closed state removes the dialog from rendering and the accessibility tree. Opening and closing are shown at the system's --opacity-disabled midpoint and --space-2 travel; open is fully settled.

Live — modality, focus trap, Escape, and focus return

Open any example, then use Tab and Shift+Tab. Focus cycles inside the modal. Escape closes and returns focus to its trigger. Confirmation overlays do not dismiss on pointer click, preserving the explicit decision; all three allow Escape because dismissal is safe here.
