Drawer / Sheet

A modal edge panel for focused supporting work. Left and right placements carry tall workflows; the bottom sheet suits compact decisions and narrow contexts. Dimensions, surface hierarchy, focus, and motion resolve entirely through tokens.css.

State matrix — placements × sizes × focus

Columns are left, right, and bottom. Rows are sm, md, and lg. Every open plate includes overlay, panel, header, title, description, scrollable content, footer, close IconButton, and composed actions. Static plates are hidden from assistive technology.

Small side drawers reuse --sidebar-width; every larger dimension is composed from --space-16. Exposed corners use --radius-xl, and every panel uses the shared popover surface, hairline, lift, and overlay layer shared with Dialog and other overlays.

Lifecycle — closed · opening · open · closing

Closed is represented by its trigger because native closed dialogs render nothing. Opening and closing use the same tokenized edge travel with different duration tokens.

Live — modality, focus trap, Escape, and focus return

Open a placement, then use Tab and Shift+Tab. Focus cycles inside; Escape or the overlay dismisses, and focus returns to the exact trigger that opened the drawer.
