Skeleton

A loading placeholder that mirrors the geometry of the content it replaces. The cream block is --card, a half-strength (--opacity-disabled) canvas glint sweeps it at calc(--duration-slow * 4) / --ease-standard, and sizes are composed from the tokens of whatever is being mirrored — never encoded. Every skeleton is aria-hidden; the surrounding region carries aria-busy and names what is loading.

Full loading state matrix — context × motion treatment

Animated is the default loading treatment. Static suppresses the sheen intentionally; reduced-motion mirrors the system media-query result so all three outcomes remain visible in one capture.

Shapes & composable sizes

Three shapes, three aliases: rectangle (base, control-height block at --radius-md), text (the body-md line box composed as calc(size × line-height) from the typography tokens, --radius-sm), circle (the Avatar scale's --avatar-size-md, --radius-full). Every dimension above is overridden inline with a token — title line, caption line, media block, lg avatar, icon-button disc — so sizes compose from the mirrored content's own tokens instead of a hardcoded ladder.

Example — text block

Line widths step down by spacing tokens (--space-16, --space-24) the way real paragraphs rag; the lead line composes the title-lg line box.

Example — avatar + text

The disc is the circle shape at its Avatar-scale default; the two lines compose the body-md and body-sm line boxes — the exact pair a loaded member row renders.

Example — card

Real Card surfaces host the placeholders. On the cream card the surface alias is composed one step darker (--_bg: var(--surface-cream-strong)) so the blocks keep their contrast; on canvas-backed surfaces the default --card fill is used as-is. Action buttons mirror as control-height rectangles, the media band as a --space-24 block.

Assistive-technology contract

Skeletons convey no information, so each one carries aria-hidden="true". The wrapper of every example on this page carries aria-busy="true" plus an aria-label naming the pending content (“Loading candidate table”, “Loading company card”) — screen readers announce one loading region, not a wall of empty blocks. When content lands, the region drops aria-busy and the skeletons leave the DOM. Under prefers-reduced-motion the sweep band is removed and the static cream blocks remain as the placeholder.
