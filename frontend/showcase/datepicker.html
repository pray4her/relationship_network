DatePicker

The ARIA date-picker-dialog pattern: a field with aria-haspopup="dialog" opens a calendar <table> of real buttons on a roving tabindex. Arrows move by day, Home/End to week edges, PageUp/PageDown by month, Enter selects, Esc closes. Every state is styled from attributes — aria-selected, data-today, data-outside, data-range — never from JS-set styles.
