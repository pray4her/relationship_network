Accordion

Every item state rendered statically and stacked in one preview so the closed and open visual systems can be compared side by side — no interaction needed. The open system is a true "selected" treatment: --selected-bg surface, a coral left indicator at --border-width-strong in --selected-border, and a rotated chevron. All values are tokens.css references; the open state is read from aria-expanded="true", never a parallel class.

Modes — single-open vs multiple-open

data-accordion="single" — opening one item collapses the rest.

An isolated workspace for one organisation, separated from every other tenant by row-level security.

Plans are billed per tenant. When a subscription lapses the tenant becomes read-only until it is renewed.

Send an invitation from the members page; the invitee accepts by email and is assigned a role.

data-accordion="multiple" — every item toggles on its own.

Register to provision a tenant; the owner role is assigned automatically.

Add members and give each one a role. Several items can stay open at once here.

Create a company profile and upload its documents to the private bucket.

Both columns are the same component — only the data-accordion attribute differs, and the difference is pure behaviour (script), not style. Try Tab to a trigger, then Enter or Space to toggle; in the single-open column the other items collapse as you go.

Content length & anatomy

One line, done.

The panel grows to fit any amount of copy because the collapse animates fractional grid rows rather than a fixed height. Long paragraphs wrap at the container measure, lists stack, and nothing is clipped once open.

A leading icon sizes to --icon-size-sm and inherits currentColor; the chevron stays pinned to the row's end by margin-inline-start: auto, and the title's min-width: 0 lets long labels wrap instead of pushing it out.

Motion & reduced motion

The panel grows and shrinks by animating grid-template-rows between 0fr and 1fr over --duration-normal on --ease-standard, while the chevron rotates over the same curve. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every transition is removed and the toggle is instant.
