Popover

A floating non-modal panel anchored to its trigger. One preview renders all four placements together with the three content recipes — simple content, header + content, and header + content + actions — so the surface system (--popover fill, --border hairline, --radius-lg, --shadow-lift, --z-popover) can be held up as the shared floating-panel recipe for the coming DropdownMenu and Dialog. The states band makes every trigger and panel state explicit, including the focused first control and a closing frame with its motion represented.

State matrix — trigger, open, focused control, closing

The trigger is a reused Button, so its default / hover / focus-visible states are the button’s own. Open settles at the --space-2 offset; the focused-first-control frame shows the shared --ring-focus ring on the panel’s first stop; closing is rendered mid-exit — half-faded at --opacity-disabled, fully retreated, its --duration-fast motion represented statically.

Live — keyboard, Escape, outside click, collision

Enter or Space opens; focus lands on the panel’s first focusable control, else the panel. Escape dismisses and returns focus to the trigger; a pointer-down outside dismisses without stealing focus. On open, script measures the trigger against the viewport and flips the side when the preferred one lacks room — open the floor-hugging trigger near the page bottom to watch it flip.
