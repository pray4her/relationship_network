Lifecycle — closed, opening, open, closing

Closed is the reference state: visibility: hidden, parked --space-1 toward the trigger, out of the accessibility tree and the hover surface. Opening enters over --duration-normal travelling that half-step; open settles at full opacity; closing exits over --duration-fast, fading as it retreats, visibility holding until the fade lands. Transitional frames are static mirrors at --opacity-disabled.

Live — hover, focus, Escape

Hover or Tab to a trigger to open; leave or blur to close. Press Escape while the trigger holds focus to dismiss without moving focus — the bubble stays gone until the pointer leaves or focus moves on, so a stale flag never outlives the trigger’s relevance.

The trigger carries aria-describedby to the bubble’s id; role="tooltip" names the relationship. Closed and closing states leave the accessibility tree via visibility: hidden, so screen readers are never left describing a bubble that isn’t there.
